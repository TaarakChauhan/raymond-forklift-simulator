# Raymond Forklift Practice Simulator

A free browser simulator for practising lift truck operation in a 3D warehouse. It runs on Three.js with physically based materials and needs no install.

Six trucks are modelled after Raymond product families: 7500 Reach Truck, 5600 Orderpicker, 4750 Sit Down Counterbalanced, 4260 Stand Up Counterbalanced, 8510 Walkie Pallet Truck and 6310 Walkie Stacker. Published Raymond figures are labelled as such in the app. Everything else is a simulator value.

Modes: Free drive, Pre shift inspection, Pick and put away, Stacking, and Timed course with scoring.

Controls: keyboard, touch and gamepad. Press H in the app for the full list.

This is a practice tool only. It is not an official Raymond product and it does not replace operator training, evaluation and certification under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178(l), Ontario Regulation 851 and CSA B335.
